BandwagonHost has two layers: the **client area** (ordering, paying, renewing) and the **KiwiVM control panel** (managing the server). Button names below follow the English interface and may change after redesigns.

## 1. Order

1. Open the [BandwagonHost order link](https://bandwagonhost.com/aff.php?aff=83651&a=add&pid=88&billingcycle=quarterly&configoption%5B17%5D=55) (the author's referral link — see below). The 1 TB plan recommended before is out of stock — no need to wait for it.
2. Confirm the plan (the one in the link has 2 TB of traffic a month and 2 GB of RAM) and Billing Cycle, and choose a **Los Angeles** option under Location.
3. Add to cart, create an account at checkout (Gmail recommended) and pay — **Alipay** (支付宝) is one of the options.

## 2. Open the KiwiVM control panel

1. Log in to the BandwagonHost client area and go to Services → My Services; your new server is listed.
2. Open it and find the entry to the **KiwiVM Control Panel**.

## 3. Find the three things to write down

- **IP address** and **SSH port**: shown on the KiwiVM home page (Main controls). BandwagonHost's SSH port **isn't always 22** — go by the page.
- **root password**: in the server-ready email; if you can't find it, generate a new one in KiwiVM with "Root password modification" (shown once — copy it immediately).
- **VEID and API Key** (optional, recommended): on the **API** page in KiwiVM's left menu. Give them to the AI when you deploy, and your apps read the traffic quota, usage and reset day straight from the dashboard, so 1 TB and 2 TB plans both show correctly. See [Traffic](/en/howto/traffic-and-ip/) for where to find them.

## 4. Check the OS

The Operating system line on the KiwiVM home page shows the current OS. BandwagonHost servers come with a default OS, so you'll usually reinstall once: click **Stop** on the home page, then **Install new OS** in the left menu, pick Ubuntu 24.04, tick the agreement and click **Reload**. A few minutes later, copy the new root password and SSH port the page shows. Step-by-step details are in [Reinstall the OS as Ubuntu](/en/howto/reinstall-os/).

<Callout type="ok">
KiwiVM shows the server Running; you've written down the IP, SSH port and root password; the OS is Ubuntu 24.04.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
BandwagonHost servers may come with their own firewall or other settings. Ignore them — step 3's scripts reconfigure everything according to the setup.
</Callout>
