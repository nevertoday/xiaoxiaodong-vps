## How to pay

**Go by what the provider's checkout page shows** — look before ordering. Common options on overseas sites:

- **Credit card** (Visa, Mastercard…): the card must allow overseas payments;
- **PayPal**: link a bank card in PayPal first;
- **Alipay** (支付宝): the recommended BandwagonHost accepts it — choose it and scan to pay; for other providers, check the checkout page.

Prices are usually in US dollars, converted at the day's rate.

## Things to watch

1. **Pay only on the provider's official site.** Never pay through personal transfers or links in chat apps.
2. For your first purchase, quarterly billing is safer; switch to yearly once you're happy.
3. Card declined? Usually overseas payments aren't enabled or the bank's risk control blocked it — a call to the bank fixes it.
4. Keep the payment email and order number for renewals, refunds and tickets.
5. Read the refund policy first; many providers only refund within a short period after purchase.

<Callout type="no">
Never give your card number or PayPal password to anyone — including people offering to "pay for you" — and never to an AI.
</Callout>

## Stuck?

<Q q="Paid, but the order says pending?">
Some providers review new orders manually, from minutes to hours. No change after a day? Open a ticket.
</Q>
<Q q="No usable payment method at checkout?">
Try another provider or another payment method (for example, sign up for PayPal first).
</Q>
